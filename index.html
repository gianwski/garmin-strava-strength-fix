Strength Workout Fixer

Fix the strength training exercises your Garmin watch got wrong, and see the right workout on Strava.

Garmin watches often misidentify strength exercises (a chest press becomes a sit-up, a lat pulldown a pull-up…). You can fix them in Garmin Connect, but those fixes never reach Strava even if the automatic sync is on. Also, if you correct the exercise in Garmin Connect the .fit file you can upload on Strava does not contain the corrected exercise names.

This page writes the correct exercises, reps and weights directly into the workout file. You then upload the corrected file to Strava, which shows the right workout. Heart rate, calories and times stay exactly as your watch recorded them.

You can see a tutorial here

Before you start:

A. Fix the exercises in Garmin app or website (optional, but recommended):

  • App: open the activity, go to the list of exercises / sets, edit the wrong exercise and choose the correct name. Save.
  • Browser: open the activity on connect.garmin.com, click the name of the wrong exercise in the exercise list and choose the correct one. Save.

B. Download the files

  1. Open your activities on Garmin Connect and open the strength workout.
  2. Tap on gear icon and then tap on "Export File" (a .zip file should be downloaded). Then, if you have done step A, tap on "Exports Splits on CSV" (a .csv file should be downloaded).

Then load the .zip below, plus the .csv if you have it. Without the .csv you fix the exercises in step 3.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't my Garmin Connect exercise edits show up on Strava?

Garmin Connect saves your edits only in its own database. The workout file (FIT) that the watch sent to Strava still contains the exercises the watch detected, and Garmin never sends the file again. Disconnecting and reconnecting Strava does not re-sync old activities.

Does exporting the FIT file from Garmin Connect include my corrections?

No. Export File gives you the file .fit exactly as recorded by the watch, with the original exercise detection. This page writes your corrections into that file.

How do I fix wrong exercises of a Garmin strength workout on Strava?

Download the original file from Garmin Connect, load it on this page, correct the exercises (or import the CSV with your Garmin Connect corrections), download the corrected file, delete the original activity on Strava and upload the new file at strava.com/upload.

Will I lose heart rate, calories or other data?

No. Only the exercise, reps and weight of each set are changed. Everything else in the file stays exactly as your watch recorded it.

Do I need a Strava subscription or to install anything?

No. The page runs in your browser on phone or computer, and the manual upload on strava.com works with a free account. Your files are never sent to any server.

Are my files uploaded somewhere? Who can see them?

No. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which only delivers the files of the page (HTML, JavaScript, an image) to your browser. It cannot run code on a server and cannot receive files. When you pick your .zip and .csv, your browser opens them on your device, the page modifies them in memory, and the corrected file is saved directly to your device. Nobody, including the author, ever sees your data. The only thing downloaded while you use the page is Garmin's list of exercises, and nothing is sent back.

How can I check that my files are not sent anywhere?

The code is open source: you can read it on GitHub. You can also open your browser's developer tools (Network tab) while using the page: you will see no request carrying your files. After the page has loaded, it keeps working even if you turn off the internet connection.

Why does Strava say the activity is a duplicate?

The original activity uploaded by Garmin is still on Strava. Delete it first, then upload the corrected file.
