Garmin watches often recognise strength exercises wrong (a chest press becomes a sit-up, a lat pulldown becomes a pull-up). You can fix them in Garmin Connect, but the fixes never reach Strava: Strava keeps the original file sent by the watch, and Garmin never sends it again.

This page writes the correct exercises, reps and weights directly into the workout file. You then upload the corrected file to Strava, which shows the right workout. Heart rate, calories and times stay exactly as your watch recorded them.

Before you start: prepare the files in Garmin Connect

A. Fix the exercises in Garmin Connect (optional, but then you fix them only once):

  • App: open the activity, go to the list of exercises / sets, tap the wrong exercise and choose the correct one. Save.
  • Browser: open the activity on connect.garmin.com, click the name of the wrong exercise in the exercise list and choose the correct one. Save.

B. Download the files (only from the browser, it works on the phone too):

  1. Open your activities on Garmin Connect and open the strength workout.
  2. Click the gear icon at the top right, then Export Original: you get a .zip file.
  3. Only if you fixed the exercises in step A: from the same menu choose Export to CSV. You get the list of exercises with your corrections. If you did not fix them in Garmin Connect, skip this: the .csv would only contain the wrong exercises.

Then load the .zip below, plus the .csv if you have it. Without the .csv you fix the exercises in step 3.

Everything runs in your browser: your files are never uploaded anywhere.
